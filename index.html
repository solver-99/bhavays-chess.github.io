Chess Mechanics & Rules

This engine fully enforces official chess guidelines:

  • Castling: King and Rook double move when unhindered.
  • En Passant: Special pawn capturing technique.
  • Pawn Promotion: Choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight upon reaching rank 8.
  • Check Warning: Glowing red visual alerts when Kings are endangered.

Master Strategy Tips

Improve your game performance with these principles:

  • Control the Center: Dominate squares e4, d4, e5, d5 early.
  • Develop Pieces: Bring Knights and Bishops into play before attacking.
  • King Safety: Castle early to protect your King behind pawn shields.
